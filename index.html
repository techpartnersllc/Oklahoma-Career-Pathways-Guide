Oklahoma's Tech Moment

With $10B+ in data center investment from Meta, Google, Cerebras, and others now underway, Oklahoma is building one of the fastest-growing tech infrastructure ecosystems in the country. Every career track below connects to real, funded demand.

Career Tracks by Role Type

Each track maps to real demand in the Tulsa/Oklahoma market. Roles are organized from entry-friendly to more specialized — but every track has an accessible on-ramp.

🖥️

Data Center Operations

Oklahoma is adding significant capacity through Meta (East Tulsa), Google (Pryor and Stillwater), and more. These roles typically do not require a four-year degree.

Typical Entry Salaries in Oklahoma (2026)

Senior Specialist$76,300+

Source: DC Geeks Data Center Salary Report 2026

Key Roles

  • Data Center Technician I/II — Hardware installation, rack/stack, break-fix, monitoring
  • Critical Facilities Technician — MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) operations
  • Data Center Operations Manager — Shift supervision, incident management
  • Commissioning Agent — Testing and verification of electrical/mechanical systems

How to Get In

  • Tulsa Tech — IT, Network Support, and Enterprise Technologies programs
  • America's Workforce Academy (AWA) — Skilled trades fast-track tied to data center construction
  • Electrical Training Alliance — Apprenticeship path into critical facilities roles
🌐

Information Technology (IT) Infrastructure

Strong demand from both enterprise employers and data center operators. Roles sit at the intersection of networking, systems, and security.

Key Roles

  • Network Engineer / Administrator — Routing, switching, LAN/WAN, SD-WAN
  • Systems Administrator — Windows/Linux server management, virtualization
  • Cloud Engineer (Azure / AWS / GCP) — Cloud architecture, IaC, hybrid environments
  • DevOps / Platform Engineer — CI/CD pipelines, Docker/Kubernetes, automation
  • IT Support Technician / Help Desk — Tier 1–3 support, endpoint management

Certifications That Open Doors

  • CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+
  • Cisco CCNA
  • Microsoft Azure Administrator (AZ-104)
  • AWS Cloud Practitioner / Solutions Architect
  • Google IT Support Professional Certificate Free via OSU/OU

Cybersecurity

One of the highest-demand fields in Oklahoma and nationally. The TCC Cyber Skills Center is the most accessible accelerated on-ramp in Tulsa.

Key Roles

  • Information Security Analyst — Risk assessment, compliance, vulnerability management
  • Cybersecurity Engineer — Network security, firewall/IDS/IPS, zero trust
  • Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker — Offensive security, red team operations

Skilled Trades — Data Center Construction & Facilities

Oklahoma's data center construction boom is creating immediate demand for licensed and apprentice-level tradespeople. Well-paying, union-backed careers.

Key Roles

  • Electrician / Journeyman Electrician — Primary feeder role for data center MEP work
  • HVAC / Mechanical Technician — Cooling systems critical to data center uptime
  • Low-Voltage / Structured Cabling Technician — Fiber, copper, and network infrastructure
  • Plumber / Pipefitter — Liquid cooling systems, water distribution
  • Welder / Ironworker — Structural buildout during construction phases

Path to Entry

  • Electrical Training Alliance — Registered DOL apprenticeships; earn while you learn (starting ~$17.71/hr, no tuition)
  • America's Workforce Academy (AWA) — Free 4-week intensive with guaranteed job offer at completion

Already employed in IT or a related field? These programs are designed to layer new credentials onto existing experience without quitting your job.

Oklahoma's statewide microcredential platform connects working professionals with short-term, skills-based programs at colleges and universities across the state.

  • Relevant tracks: Computer Networking, IT Fundamentals, AI Literacy, Cybersecurity, Programming
  • Format: Weeks to months; online, hybrid, in-person
  • Cost: Varies; some programs no-cost or under $500
  • State backing: $150,000 in 2025 grants for AI-focused microcredentials
upskillok.org →

America's Workforce Academy (AWA) — Meta

Meta launched AWA in response to massive demand (35,000 applications in 7 days) for skilled trades workers to build its data center infrastructure — including the new AI-optimized data center in East Tulsa that broke ground in April 2026.

What It Is

Nationwide, fast-track training for skilled trades. Zero cost. Meta funds everything: tuition, airfare, lodging, and a daily stipend.

Who It's For

Veterans, recent graduates, career changers, and new entrants. No prior experience required — open to all 50 states.

What You Get

NCCER certification + U.S. Workforce Certificate + guaranteed job offer through a Meta contractor partner upon graduation.

Status

Pilot in Louisiana, Ohio, Indiana, and Texas — Oklahoma expansion anticipated as East Tulsa data center buildout accelerates.

Internships in Oklahoma

The most important advice: identify companies you're interested in, then go directly to their careers page — or simply reach out and inquire. Many of Oklahoma's most valuable internship programs are never posted on LinkedIn or Indeed. They live on company career portals and fill months before summer starts.

The Right Strategy

  1. Pick an industry (energy, finance, tech, healthcare, government, tribal, nonprofit)
  2. List major Oklahoma companies in that industry — Google "[industry] companies Oklahoma" or use Oklahoma Chamber of Commerce directories
  3. Go directly to each company's careers page — search "intern" or "student" in their job portal
  4. If nothing is posted, reach out directly — find the HR or recruiting contact on LinkedIn and send a brief, professional email
  5. Follow the company on LinkedIn — most announce internship windows on their company feed
  6. Reach out to university alumni at that company — alumni are almost always willing to make a warm introduction
