About This Guide

TulsaCareerPath is a free public resource built and maintained by Tech Partners LLC — Tulsa's IT staffing and recruitment specialists. The goal is simple: surface what already exists in Tulsa's workforce ecosystem, make pathways obvious, and give people a concrete plan rather than a list of links to sort through.

For occupations not covered in the six tracks above, CareerOneStop (U.S. Dept. of Labor) offers the most comprehensive free career exploration tool available — including a validated interest assessment, live wage data for every occupation, and a training finder with WIOA-funded program flags.

This is a living document. Programs, employers, and opportunities are added as they emerge. Last reviewed: June 2026.
